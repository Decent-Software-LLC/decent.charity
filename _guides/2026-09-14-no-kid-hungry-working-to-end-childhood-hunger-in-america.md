**No Kid Hungry: Working to End Childhood Hunger in America**
====================================================================================

No Kid Hungry is a national campaign dedicated to ending childhood hunger in the United States. Run by Share Our Strength, the organization works to ensure every child has access to the food they need to grow up healthy and strong.

## Organization Background
No Kid Hungry was launched in 2010 by Share Our Strength after 25 years of investing in local nonprofits and developing effective approaches to poverty and hunger. As a child hunger organization, its primary focus is ending childhood hunger, while Share Our Strength continues to support other initiatives. The campaign is guided by values that include being bold, mobilizing people’s strengths, embracing diversity, staying inventive, and finding joy in the work.

## Communities Served
No Kid Hungry serves children and families experiencing hunger across the United States. The organization’s efforts are directed at childhood hunger broadly, supporting local communities through a network of partner organizations that deliver food and resources where they are needed most.

## Programs and Services
The organization’s core work includes:
- **School Meals** – supporting access to nutritious food during the school day  
- **Summer Meals** – providing meals when school is not in session  
- **Feeding Kids at Home** – helping families obtain food for home consumption  
- **Grocery Benefits** – assisting with access to affordable groceries  
- **Where Our Grants Go** – granting funds to local nonprofits that implement hunger‑relief solutions  

These programs are national in scope but are carried out locally by partner organizations that receive funding, training, and technical assistance from No Kid Hungry.
