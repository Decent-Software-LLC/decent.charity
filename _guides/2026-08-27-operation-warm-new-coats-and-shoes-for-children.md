**Operation Warm: New Coats and Shoes for Children**  
================================================================================

Operation Warm provides brand‑new winter coats, athletic‑style shoes, and sports bras to children living in great need across the United States. By delivering these basic clothing essentials, the organization aims to relieve hardship and foster warmth, confidence, and hope for the children and families it serves.

## Organization Background  
Operation Warm describes its mission as providing the gift of warmth, confidence, and hope through donations of clothing to children in need. The organization manufactures brand‑new, high‑quality coats and shoes and partners with compassionate individuals, community groups, and corporations to distribute these items. It is registered as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and all donations are tax‑deductible to the extent allowed by law.

## Communities Served  
The organization serves children who lack basic clothing essentials, focusing on those in Title 1 schools and school districts, Head Start programs, homeless shelters, and other beneficiary organizations with urgent need. Its reach extends nationwide, targeting children living in poverty throughout the United States.

## Programs and Services  
Operation Warm’s principal work is the production and distribution of brand‑new coats, athletic‑style shoes, and sports bras for children in need. As a national nonprofit, it coordinates a network of partners across the country to deliver these items locally through schools, shelters, and community organizations.
