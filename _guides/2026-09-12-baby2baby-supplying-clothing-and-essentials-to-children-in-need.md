**Baby2Baby: Supplying Clothing and Essentials to Children in Need**
====================================================================

Baby2Baby is a national nonprofit that provides children in need with diapers, clothing, and other basic necessities. By partnering with local organizations across the country, the group helps families allocate limited resources toward food and shelter.

## Organization Background
Baby2Baby describes itself as a national nonprofit focused on delivering essential items such as diapers, clothing, and formula to children who lack them. The organization operates by supplying these goods to a network of partner agencies rather than distributing directly to individuals.

## Communities Served
Baby2Baby reaches more than one million children each year in all 50 states. Its essentials are distributed to nearly 1,000 nonprofit organizations and government agencies, including:
- Foster care agencies  
- Resource centers & wraparound services  
- School districts & educational programs  
- Homeless shelters & outreach programs  
- Domestic violence programs  
- Children’s hospitals networks & clinics  
- Diaper banks  
- Disaster relief organizations  

## Programs and Services
The core program consists of gathering and distributing basic necessities—diapers, clothing, formula, and similar items—to children in need through its nationwide partner network. Services are provided nationally via the described affiliate organizations.
