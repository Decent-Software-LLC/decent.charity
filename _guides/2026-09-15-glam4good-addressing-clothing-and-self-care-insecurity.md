**GLAM4GOOD: Addressing Clothing and Self-Care Insecurity**
================================================================================

GLAM4GOOD is a nonprofit that repurposes unused merchandise to address clothing and self‑care insecurity in the United States. By diverting industry excess, the organization aims to bolster self‑esteem and empower dignity for people in need. Learn more about its work on the [GLAM4GOOD official website](https://glam4good.com/).

## Organization Background
The source does not provide specific founding details; readers should confirm the organization’s history directly with GLAM4GOOD. Its mission, as stated on the website, is to “bolster self‑esteem & empower dignity while diverting industry excess to create opportunities for people in need.” GLAM4GOOD operates as an innovative nonprofit that collects unused clothing and self‑care products from industry partners and redistributes them to individuals facing insecurity.

## Communities Served
GLAM4GOOD serves people across the United States who experience clothing and self‑care insecurity. The organization highlights impact pillars in disaster recovery, child & family welfare, social equity, and mental health & wellbeing. Events and pop‑up shops are held in Northern California, Southern California, New York, Illinois, Michigan, and Texas. Through partnerships—such as with the NYC Administration of Children’s Services—GLAM4GOOD delivers clothing, self‑care items, and school essentials to students nationwide, and it ran a COVID‑19 critical aid initiative that reported a 4 million‑impact total.

## Programs and Services
The principal work of GLAM4GOOD involves repurposing unused merchandise (clothing and self‑care products) to address insecurity. This is accomplished through:
- Local events and pop‑up shops in the states listed above  
- Nationwide delivery of clothing, self‑care, and school essentials to students in need  
- The COVID‑19 critical aid initiative  
These activities reflect both locally focused services and broader national outreach.
