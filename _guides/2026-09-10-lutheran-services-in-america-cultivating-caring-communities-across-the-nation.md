**Lutheran Services in America: Cultivating Caring Communities Across the Nation**  
====================================================================

Lutheran Services in America is a national nonprofit network that brings together Lutheran health and human services organizations to advance health and opportunity for all. Through policy advocacy, innovation, and collective action, the network supports children, youth, families, older adults, people with disabilities, veterans, and others in need across the United States.

## Organization Background  
Founded in 1997, Lutheran Services in America was created by 300 Lutheran health and human services nonprofit organizations spanning 1,400 communities, together with the Evangelical Lutheran Church in America (ELCA) and The Lutheran Church—Missouri Synod (LCMS). Headquartered in Washington, DC with offices on Capitol Hill, the organization serves as a national network that mobilizes leaders, partners, and funders to drive policy and systems change. Its mission is to cultivate caring communities that advance health and opportunity for all. The network’s strategy focuses on growing collective capacity, catalyzing innovation and collaboration, and amplifying a united faith‑based voice that shares effective solutions.

## Communities Served  
The Lutheran Services in America network reaches approximately one in 50 people in the United States each year. It serves a broad range of populations, including children and youth, families, older adults, people with intellectual and developmental disabilities, veterans, refugees, and others experiencing need. Services are delivered through local member organizations in 1,400 communities nationwide, while the national office coordinates advocacy, innovation, and capacity‑building efforts.

## Programs and Services  
The network’s principal work is organized into several program areas:  
- **Children, Youth & Families** – supporting better outcomes for young people and their families.  
- **Older Adults** – improving independence and choice for seniors.  
- **Health Through Housing** – integrating health services with stable housing.  
- **People with Disabilities** – championing meaningful services and support for individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities.  
- **Social Sector Leadership** – strengthening leadership and capacity within the social service sector.  
- **Public Policy & Advocacy** – advancing policy changes that affect the populations served.  

These programs are implemented locally by the 300 member organizations; the national office provides coordination, advocacy, and support for systemic change.
