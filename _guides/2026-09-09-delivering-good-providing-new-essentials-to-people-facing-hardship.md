**Delivering Good: Providing New Essentials to People Facing Hardship**  
====================================================================

Delivering Good connects individuals and families facing hardship with new, essential items and services through a network of community partners. Founded in 1985, the organization has provided more than $3 billion worth of donated new merchandise free of charge to people dealing with poverty, disaster, and other challenges. Its mission is to help people move forward with dignity, hope, and opportunity by matching them with needed goods and support.

## Organization Background
Delivering Good was founded in 1985 and operates as a tax‑exempt 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Its mission states that it helps people facing hardship and disaster move forward by working with partners to match them with new, essential items and services—providing care that supports dignity, renews hope, and opens doors to opportunity. The organization’s vision is a future where everyone rises above hardship with dignity, hope, and opportunity, fueled by collective impact. Core values guiding its work include Dignity, Encouragement, Care, Belonging, Resourcefulness, Resiliency, and Accountability. Delivering Good is recognized by Charity Navigator as a 4‑Star charity, earning a perfect score for Impact & Measurement and Leadership & Adaptability.

## Communities Served
Delivering Good serves families and individuals experiencing a range of social challenges, including poverty, homelessness, workforce reentry, foster care, and impacts from natural disasters or humanitarian crises. The organization works with local and national community partners to identify needs and deliver appropriate new essentials. While the source does not specify geographic limits, the emphasis on partnering with local organizations suggests services are delivered at the community level through a national network.

## Programs and Services
The organization’s principal work involves procuring and distributing new, essential products through its partner network. Product categories mentioned include shoes, apparel, home goods, backpacks, hats & gloves, and socks. Delivering Good also offers specialized packs such as the Basic Comforts Pack. In addition to goods, the organization supports disaster relief efforts and runs initiatives like the Women of Impact series, the Women of Impact Summit, and the An Evening for Delivering Good Gala. All services are facilitated in collaboration with community partners, and the model emphasizes matching donors’ contributions with verified local needs.
