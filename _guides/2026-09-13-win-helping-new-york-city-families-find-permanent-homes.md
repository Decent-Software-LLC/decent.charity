**Win: Helping New York City Families Find Permanent Homes**
====================================================

Win is the largest provider of family shelter and supportive housing in New York City. It works to move families from shelter to permanent housing by offering essential services that address the root causes of homelessness. All of its programs are guided by the mission to support families in attaining stable, long‑term homes.

## Organization Background
Win began as an emergency shelter for four homeless women and their children and has grown into the city’s leading provider of family shelter and supportive housing. Its mission is to support families in shelter to attain permanent housing by providing essential services and resources that tackle systemic inequities at the core of the homelessness crisis. The organization’s vision is to break the cycle of homelessness by being a trusted leader in innovative solutions for families in need. Win operates 16 shelters and maintains over 450 units of permanent supportive housing across New York City.

## Communities Served
Win serves homeless families throughout New York City, with shelters located in Manhattan, the Bronx, Brooklyn, and Queens. Each night, Win houses more than 6,500 people, including over 3,000 children. Approximately 967 families were placed into permanent housing by Win in the last year. About nine out of ten families served are headed by women, and more than 12,400 individuals have called Win “home” over the past year.

## Programs and Services
Win provides two main types of housing paired with supportive services:
- **Transitional shelter housing** – 16 shelters offering individual family units for short‑term stays.
- **Permanent supportive housing** – Over 450 units that include ongoing counseling and other services to promote long‑term stability.

All services are delivered locally in New York City and are designed to help families achieve permanent housing through a holistic approach that addresses health, employment, education, and other needs.
